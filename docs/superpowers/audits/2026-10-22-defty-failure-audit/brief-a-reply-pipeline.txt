SCOPE A — Defty reply pipeline and LLM runtime.
How a human message reaches Defty and how the reply is produced, streamed, persisted and recovered. Start from apps/api/src/lib/dispatch-agent-message.ts, apps/api/src/workers/handlers/agent-reply.ts, apps/api/src/lib/agent-runner.ts, apps/api/src/lib/agent-stream-loop.ts, apps/api/src/lib/agent-llm.ts, apps/api/src/routes/agent.ts, apps/api/src/routes/messages.ts (mention/DM routing), ensure-defty-membership / ensure-defty-dm helpers, and the job queue/worker wiring they use. Look for: messages that never get a reply, double replies, stuck "thinking" states, provider misconfiguration handling (no provider, bad key, rate limit, timeout, model not found), streaming/SSE breakage, tool-loop limits and runaway loops, token/context overflow, retries that duplicate side effects, error messages leaking secrets/stack traces, race conditions between concurrent messages in one space, and how failures are shown to the user.
