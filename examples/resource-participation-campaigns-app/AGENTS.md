# Resource Participation Campaigns App

- Build only through the public App v0 and current declarative Module contract.
- Keep this App independently installable from Contacts; never copy Contact
  records or assume its installation/database IDs.
- Phase 4 may relate a Campaign to Contacts only through the host Resource
  contract introduced in Module v2.
- Do not invoke the sandbox email provider in Phase 4.
- Do not add executable code, connector authority, automation, custom UI,
  runtime, sync, or public routes.
