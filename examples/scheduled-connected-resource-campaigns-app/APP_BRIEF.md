# Scheduled Connected Resource Campaigns

This is the Track A Protocol v2 upgrade of the independent connected Campaigns
proof. It preserves the App, Module, dependency, action, and provider contracts
and adds one requested-only daily Campaign send declaration.

The host owns the schedule, timezone, exact Campaign and Contact pins, budgets,
validity, policy, approval, claims, Runs, receipts, and kill switches. The App
cannot execute the request by staging it or choose any of those authority
inputs. This proof does not claim workflows, bulk fan-out, custom UI, runtime,
sync, or public ingress.
